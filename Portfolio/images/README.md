# Images Folder

## Profile Photo
Place your profile photo here as: `profile.jpg`

The photo will be displayed in the hero section as a circular, comic-panel-framed photo.

### Recommended Specs
- Format: JPG or WebP
- Dimensions: At least 400×400px (square crop works best)
- The image is displayed in a circular frame with a cyan border and orange drop shadow

If `profile.jpg` is missing or fails to load, the site automatically shows your initials "FS" in a stylish placeholder.
